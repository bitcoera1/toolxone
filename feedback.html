💬 ToolXone Feedback

Help Shape the Future of ToolXone

Your suggestions, ideas and experience help us improve existing tools, identify problems and decide what useful tools to build next.

💡 Suggest a New Tool

Have an idea for a calculator, converter, utility or future digital tool? We'd love to hear it.

Tell us what kind of tool you would find useful and, if possible, explain what problem it would solve or which features you would like it to include.

🛠️ Report an Issue

If you discover an error, incorrect calculation, broken feature or unexpected behaviour, please let us know so we can investigate and improve the affected tool.

⭐ Share Your Experience

We'd love to hear what you enjoy about ToolXone and where you think we can improve.

Your experience can help us understand what works well, identify areas that need improvement and build better tools for everyone.

📝 What to Include

Providing a few relevant details can help us understand your feedback and respond more effectively.

  • For tool suggestions: describe the tool you would like to see and what problem it would solve.
  • For bug reports: mention the tool name, what you were trying to do, what happened and what you expected to happen.
  • For general feedback: tell us what you liked, what could be improved or what would make the experience more useful.
  • For technical issues: including the browser or device you were using can be helpful when investigating the problem.

🔒 Please Don't Include Sensitive Information

Please do not include passwords, payment information, financial account credentials or other sensitive personal information in your feedback.

Only provide the information needed to explain your suggestion, experience or technical issue.
