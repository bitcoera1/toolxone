ToolXone is committed to respecting your privacy and being transparent about how information may be handled when you use our website, calculators, converters and utility tools.

Last Updated: November 15, 2026

📋 Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes information handling associated with the ToolXone website. This notice does not itself record a consent choice for analytics, cookies, or advertising technologies.

ToolXone does not currently require users to create an account or register in order to use its free online tools. We aim to collect and use information only for legitimate operational, analytical, communication, security and improvement purposes.

📊 Information We May Collect

The information associated with ToolXone may vary depending on how you use the website.

ToolXone records tool-use information, including tool identifiers or names and usage statistics. Some of this information is stored in the browser, and tool-use information is sent to the ToolXone API for aggregate statistics, as described below.

ToolXone pages also load third-party analytics and advertising code and make requests to external APIs and libraries. The information those providers process depends on their code and configuration; those details have not been established by inspection of ToolXone's source code.

When a feedback or public-review submission is sent, ToolXone's Worker may derive a country code from the request and store it with the submission. Information visitors choose to provide is described below.

✉️ Information You Voluntarily Provide

The feedback form on ToolXone tools can submit a tool identifier and name, rating, message, feedback category for private feedback, submission type, name and optional email address. The form defaults to private feedback. A visitor may instead select public review and submit the required publication-consent choice.

If you contact ToolXone by email, you choose what information to include in your message.

When a feedback or public-review submission is sent, the Worker may derive a country code from Cloudflare request metadata and store it with the submission when available.

Private feedback is not included in public-review responses. For public reviews, the Worker records the publication-consent version and timestamp and includes a review in public responses only when the required consent fields and published status are present. The current implementation can display the submitted name (or Anonymous), rating, message, tool, server-derived country when available, and date. Email is not included in the public-review responses generated by the current implementation. Publication consent applies to displaying a public review; it is not consent for advertising, cookies, or general tracking.

Please provide only information that is necessary for your inquiry, suggestion, feedback or issue report.

📈 Tool Usage & Platform Statistics

ToolXone stores a local statistics object in browser local storage. It includes aggregate totals and per-tool information such as the tool identifier, name, category, action count, and first- and last-use timestamps.

Tool actions also send the tool identifier and name, and separate aggregate counter keys, to the ToolXone Worker. The Worker maintains aggregate counts and server-generated last-use or update times. The application payloads inspected for this policy do not include the local first- or last-use timestamps or a visitor identifier.

You can clear local statistics through your browser's site-data settings. The inspected code contains a reset function but no visitor-facing control for it.

These code-level measures do not establish all hosting, infrastructure, account, or provider security settings. No method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please avoid sending sensitive information that is not necessary for your inquiry or feedback.
