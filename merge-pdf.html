      Private browser processing

      Your selected PDF contents are processed locally in this browser and are not uploaded to ToolXone for merging. The page still loads the PDF library from a CDN and may make separate requests for site functions such as analytics or advertising.

      How to Merge PDF Files

      Combine multiple PDF documents into one file with ToolXone Merge PDF. Add files, arrange their order, and merge them in your browser.

      Arrange PDF Files

      Add at least two supported PDF files. Use Move Up and Move Down to set the order of documents and their pages in the merged output.

      Merge PDFs in Your Browser

      ToolXone validates selected PDF content and combines files locally in the browser. The initial tool limits are 10 files, 25 MiB per file, and 100 MiB combined.

      Unsupported or Protected Files

      Malformed, encrypted, password-protected, or otherwise unsupported PDFs may not be mergeable. The tool identifies files it cannot read and explains the issue by filename.
